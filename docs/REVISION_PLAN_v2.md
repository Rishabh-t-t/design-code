## 1. Live Twin: one engine, three uses

All three twins are the same app (your HTML) running in a frame without a border, in one of three **embed modes**. The page sets the mode when it creates the frame (`window.CX_EMBED = { mode }`). The standalone twin file is unchanged.

| Mode | Where | What shows | Hidden | Who drives it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hero` | Hero | 3D building, the ConnectX top bar, 3–4 floating KPI cards (load, solar, battery, grid), a small asset card when an asset is clicked, and +/− zoom buttons | Loader, side rails, decision log, scenario chips, alert card, the bottom timeline, weather badge | The reader (drag, click, zoom) |
| `scenario` | 03 Scenarios | 3D building, a slim top bar, KPI cards and the current scenario card | Everything else in the list above, plus all controls | The page: as each area becomes active, the page tells the twin which scenario to play |
| `ask` | Peak-price block | The twin's own Home view with the ask box, then its resolver, then the workspace with the twin playing **Peak tariff** | Side rails and chips | The page: it types the question when the block comes into view; a Replay button reruns it |

**Shared behaviour in every mode:**
- **Scroll is never trapped.** Inside a twin, the mouse wheel and trackpad scroll are passed back to the page, so the page keeps scrolling smoothly. Zoom moves to the +/− buttons and trackpad pinch (pinch reaches the page as Ctrl+wheel, so it's easy to tell apart).
- **Drag** rotates and **click** selects, as in the standalone twin.
- **No Explore or Exit step**, and no shield over the twin.
- **Costs stay at one twin's worth:**
  - A frame is created about one screen before its section is reached.
  - It pauses (stops drawing) whenever it is off screen.
  - It is unloaded when it is more than about three screens away, and recreated with its picture as you come back.
  - Only one twin draws at a time.
- **Instant picture for every twin:** each mode gets a still of its first frame, generated by the build script with Playwright and embedded as compressed WebP (about 40–80 KB each). It shows instantly and the live twin fades in over it, so nothing flashes or jumps.

## 3. Sticky scenes, rebuilt (fixes the snap)

**Cause:** the scenes are pinned with GSAP's pin, which moves the element into a fixed position on its own timing. That fights Lenis's smoothed scroll, so the scene arrives late and snaps.

**Fix:** use the browser's own `position: sticky`.
- Each scene becomes a tall track (choice ≈ 250vh, scenarios ≈ 400vh) with a sticky child at `top: 96px`.
- The scene then stops exactly at its line and holds there, exactly as smooth as normal scrolling.
- ScrollTrigger only reads progress through the track (no pin) to drive the beats and switch scenarios.
- Reduced motion and no-JS stay unchanged: the content is simply laid out, with nothing sticky.

## 4. Design system refinements

**Type scale v2** (1024 → 1920):

| Role | Size |
|---|---|
| Display | 88 → 136 |
| Summary heading | 40 → 56 |
| Chapter title | 40 → 56 |
| Statement | 28 → 40 |
| Label | 17 → 20 |
| Body | 16 → 17 |
| Small body (intro paragraphs) | 14 → 15 |
| Meta | 12 |

Display letter-spacing is tightened slightly, and `text-wrap: balance` goes on every heading.

**Spacing:**
- Space between sections rises to `clamp(160px, 14vw, 280px)`.
- Inside a chapter: label → title 20 px, title → body 48 px.

**Lines:** default hairlines become `#E7E7E3`. The darker `#DCDCD8` is reserved for active or structural lines only.

**Colour usage:**
- **Tcules green** for highlights only: the link marker, the "Live" pill, focus rings and the footer.
- **ConnectX blue and orange** only inside the product (twins and role cards).
- Page text stays in neutral inks.

**Surfaces:**
- Product shows on the page background without boxes.
- White cards are kept only where a card is the object (role cards, testimonials, the BuildTwin card).

All of this still passes AA contrast; it will be rechecked in R6.

## 6. Risks and how they're handled

- **Wheel pass-through smoothness:** wheel deltas from the twin feed straight into Lenis, so scrolling over a twin feels the same as anywhere else. If any choppiness shows up in testing, I'll tune it in R3 before going further.
- **Hero performance:** the instant picture keeps first paint fast. The 3D library starts downloading early but at low priority, so it doesn't hold up the page text.
- **Camera moves in scenario mode:** the twin's own camera "fly-to" moves stay on (they explain the scenario). They pause whenever the twin is off screen.
- **The early-concepts embedded images** (IMP-09) and the placeholder metric (IMP-03) are unchanged by this plan.
