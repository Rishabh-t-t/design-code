Light and the stage

Time is shown through light

The page never says how long it took. The light moves from dawn (the idea) through day (the build) to dusk (the live product), and the stage is built from layers that move at different speeds.

Parallax depth. Five layers, each moving at its own speed relative to scroll. The building is the anchor at 1×; UI and copy drift slightly faster, so they float in front of it.

The twin

One building in three looks

The progression is the backbone of the story. Each look morphs into the next in place, on the same stage, so it reads as one object growing up.

Sketch

Pencil lines on a faint grid, with handwritten notes around it. Loose and fast.

2.5D

Realistic render

Glass, materials and real light. Highlight and dim still work on top. Mood only: your new render replaces this.

What's real and what's a stand-in

Stand-ins

  • The realistic render is a colour grade of today's 2.5D picture. Your new render replaces it.
  • The sketch is generated from the same picture. Real sketches will be drawn.
  • The headline is a placeholder.

Assumed

  • The question bar, glass panels and pins as the product's UI language.
  • Scenario wall as 8 × 3 tiles.
  • Readings in the UI (318 kW, 81%) are sample values.

Your call

  • Smoke glass or light glass at dusk.
  • How dark the dusk is.
  • Whether the rail shows its labels or only marks.
  • Which references feel closest.
